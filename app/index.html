Roadmap to Platinum

One trophy can be missed. Just one.

Grand Theft Auto VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Once a story mission is done, you cannot select it again. One meditation spot — Lucia's balcony in Chapter 1 — disappears when that chapter ends, and with it goes the trophy Positive Mental Attitude, and the platinum with it.

Save discipline — four rules

  1. Save at the start of every chapter. Make a manual save the moment a new chapter opens, before you do anything else.
  2. Meditate at Lucia's balcony before Chapter 1 ends. After the mission Down to Earth, follow Lucia to the balcony in the morning and accept the meditation.
  3. Keep a save from just before the final mission. Never overwrite it — it is your only way back to the story's closing stretch.
  4. Never use cheats on your trophy save. Cheats switch off trophies and saving.
