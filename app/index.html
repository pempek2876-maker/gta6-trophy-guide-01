One trophy can be missed for good

There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus in GTA VI. If you pass a missable moment, the only way back is a second full playthrough — or a manual save made in time.

Save discipline — four habits

  1. Make a manual save at the start of every chapter, before playing a single mission.
  2. Meditate at Lucia’s balcony before Chapter 1 ends — that spot is gone once the chapter closes.
  3. Keep a manual save from just before the final mission.
  4. Never overwrite your chapter-start save with an autosave-only slot.
